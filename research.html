Research

Privacy-preserving, decentralized AI for healthcare

My work centers on learning from heterogeneous, multimodal healthcare data distributed across institutions and devices, without unnecessarily centralizing sensitive information. That throughline runs across four areas:

01

Trustworthy & Secure AI

Trustworthy AI, AI security and robustness, privacy-preserving AI — building systems whose behavior can be trusted and verified, not just measured on benchmark accuracy.

02

Federated & Decentralized Learning

Federated learning, federated edge AI, secure and decentralized ML, non-IID and personalized federated learning across heterogeneous clients.

03

Healthcare AI

Multimodal healthcare AI, healthcare foundation models, AI for digital health, multimodal and longitudinal clinical data.

04

Edge Intelligence

Edge intelligence and TinyML for deployment on resource-constrained devices close to the patient.

Current Research

dAIbetes: federated learning for personalized Type 2 diabetes prediction

Since joining the University of Hamburg in July 2026, I've been working on privacy-preserving federated learning and virtual-twin modeling for personalized Type 2 diabetes treatment prediction. The central research question: how can predictive models be trained across distributed, non-IID clinical data sources while keeping patient data local — and how can the resulting systems stay robust and trustworthy once deployed.

Earlier work focused on medical image segmentation — developing attention-enhanced architectures for nuclei and tissue analysis in histopathology images (see Publications and Projects).
